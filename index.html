❓ Help & FAQ
🎯 Task levels
Every 5th level is a TASK. Catch only the candy with the green ring and the ★ CATCH label (or the 🛡️/✨ power-up). Catching a wrong candy costs you 1 ❤️.
💣 Bombs
Check the bomb list in the top bar. Red ring + 💥 = instant game over, 💔 = -1 life, 📉 = target +15, 💰 = -500 score. The 🛡️ Shield saves you from one bomb.
🌍 3 Themes
Travel through the Candy Kingdom, dive into the Deep Sea and finish with Premium Coffee. When the theme changes, the old candies stop falling completely. Keep playing to discover them!
🎨 Baskets
15 baskets in total - 5 per theme. They unlock automatically as you level up, or you can unlock them early with coins from the daily spin.
🎯 Missions
Complete missions to earn coins. Open them from the 🎯 Missions button on the home screen.
🪙 Coins
You earn coins by completing levels, keeping your daily spin streak, and finishing missions. Spend them in the Basket Shop.
❄️ Game freeze?

🔊 No sound?
Check the settings.
