Privacy Policy — Candy Mass

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Mass Interactive Games

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Candy Mass game ("the Game", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

In short: you can play entirely as a Guest without giving us any personal information. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store your display name, email address, game progress and best score so they sync across your devices and appear on the leaderboard.

1. Information we collect

What When Why
Google account display name and email address Only if you tap "Sign in with Google" To identify your account, sync your progress and show your name on the leaderboard
Game progress (level, score, lives, coins, baskets, missions) While you play To save your progress and let you continue later
Best score and best level When you complete a level or finish a game To display the leaderboard
Technical data (browser type, approximate region, error logs) Automatically, by our service providers To keep the Game working, secure it and fix problems

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and we do not show third-party advertisements in the Game.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device — the Game uses your browser's local storage for settings, progress and a local copy of the leaderboard. This works offline and stays on your device.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) — if you sign in with Google, your display name, email address, game progress and best score are stored in Google Firebase, which is operated by Google LLC. This is what makes your progress sync between devices and your score appear on the global leaderboard.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security pages.

3. Leaderboard

If you sign in, your display name, best score and best level are visible to other players on the in-game leaderboard. Guests are not added to the global leaderboard; they only see scores stored on their own device. You can ask us to remove your leaderboard entry at any time using the contact address below.

4. How long we keep data

Cloud data (progress and leaderboard entry) is kept while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your data, we remove it from Firebase within 30 days. Local data stays on your device until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account — use "Play as Guest". No personal information is sent to us.
  • Sign out — use 🚪 in the Game's top bar to sign out of Google at any time.
  • Delete your data — email us and we will delete your cloud progress and leaderboard entry.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Google Firebase. Database access rules restrict each player to their own saved data and prevent leaderboard scores from being forged. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced inside the Game.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at massgms@gmail.com.
