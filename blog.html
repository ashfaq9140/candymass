About this blog

We are a very small studio — small enough that the person writing this code is also the person writing these posts. That means the blog is honest by default: no marketing language, no pretending a feature shipped when it has not.

If you are building a small game yourself, the posts about difficulty curves and testing are the ones most likely to be useful to you. If you are just here to play, everything you need is in the how to play guide.

📬 Get updates
