Reporting a bug well

Bug reports are the most useful thing you can send us, and a good one takes about thirty seconds to write. The most helpful details, in order of usefulness:

  1. What you were doing when it happened — which round, which world, whether it was a bonus round
  2. What went wrong, and what you expected instead
  3. Your device and browser, if you know them
  4. Whether it happens every time or only sometimes

If the game showed an error message, a screenshot of it is worth more than a description.

Data and privacy requests

You can ask us to delete your cloud progress and leaderboard entry at any time. Email us from the Google account you signed in with and we will remove it, normally within a few days and always within thirty.

If you have been playing as a guest, there is nothing for us to delete — your data only ever existed on your device. Clearing your browser or app data removes it immediately.

Full details of what we store and why are in the privacy policy.

Mass Interactive Games is an independent studio. Candy Mass is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no game engine — and released on the web and on Google Play.

If you are building something similar and have a question about how a particular part works, ask. We are happy to talk about it, and there is more detail in the post about building without an engine.
